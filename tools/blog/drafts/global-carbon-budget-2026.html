How much carbon budget is left for 1.5 °C, 1.7 °C and 2 °C?

A carbon budget is the total CO₂ that can still be emitted while keeping warming below a given level with a given probability. Because warming is roughly proportional to cumulative CO₂, every year of emissions is subtracted from it. Our carbon budget explainer covers how it works.
