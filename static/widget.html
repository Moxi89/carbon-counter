How it works

  • Shows CO₂ emitted worldwide so far this year, updating several times a second.
  • Uses the Global Carbon Budget 2025 figure of 42.2 billion tonnes a year (about 1,338 tonnes per second). See how the counter works.
  • Resizes to fit: works from about 260 px wide. A height of 160–180 px fits best.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts, and calm motion for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

Questions and answers

Is the CO₂ counter widget free?

Yes. The widget is free for any website, including schools, blogs, businesses and non-profits. Please keep the small credit link.

Does the widget track visitors or use cookies?

No. The widget is a small static page with a few lines of JavaScript. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts.

Where do the widget’s numbers come from?

It uses the Global Carbon Budget 2025 estimate of 42.2 billion tonnes of CO₂ a year (fossil fuels plus land-use change), spread evenly over the year: about 1,338 tonnes per second.
