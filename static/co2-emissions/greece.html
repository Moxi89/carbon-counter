Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Greece emit?

Greece emitted 53.4 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Greece’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Greece emits 5.3 tonnes per person, 1.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (10.0 million people in 2024).

Are Greece’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 2.7% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 53%. Emissions peaked in 2007 at 114.5 million tonnes and are now 53% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Greece?

Oil is the largest source, at 63% of Greece’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (33.4 million tonnes).

How much has Greece contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Greece has emitted about 4 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 46th largest historical contribution.
