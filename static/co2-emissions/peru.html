Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Peru emit?

Peru emitted 70.2 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Peru’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Peru emits 2.0 tonnes per person, 57% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (34.2 million people in 2024).

Are Peru’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 6.2% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 132%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Peru?

Oil is the largest source, at 56% of Peru’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (39.1 million tonnes).

How much has Peru contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Peru has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 66th largest historical contribution.
