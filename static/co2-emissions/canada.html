Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Canada emit?

Canada emitted 533.3 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.4% of global emissions. That is about 17 tonnes every second.

What are Canada’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Canada emits 13.4 tonnes per person, 2.8× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (39.7 million people in 2024).

Are Canada’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 2.2% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 7%. Emissions peaked in 2007 at 590.0 million tonnes and are now 10% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Canada?

Oil is the largest source, at 46% of Canada’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (244.6 million tonnes).

How much has Canada contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Canada has emitted about 36 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.9% of the world total, the 9th largest historical contribution.
