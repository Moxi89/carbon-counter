Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Belarus emit?

Belarus emitted 55.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Belarus’ CO₂ emissions per person?

Belarus emits 6.2 tonnes per person, 1.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (9.1 million people in 2024).

Are Belarus’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (+0.2% on 2023). Compared with 2005, emissions are down 6%. Emissions peaked in 1986 at 121.5 million tonnes and are now 54% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Belarus?

Gas is the largest source, at 55% of Belarus’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (30.6 million tonnes).

How much has Belarus contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Belarus has emitted about 5 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 42nd largest historical contribution.
