Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Egypt emit?

Egypt emitted 258.4 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.7% of global emissions. That is about 8 tonnes every second.

What are Egypt’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Egypt emits 2.2 tonnes per person, 53% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (116.5 million people in 2024).

Are Egypt’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 3.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 55%. Emissions peaked in 2021 at 266.8 million tonnes and are now 3% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Egypt?

Oil is the largest source, at 44% of Egypt’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (113.0 million tonnes).

How much has Egypt contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Egypt has emitted about 7 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.4% of the world total, the 32nd largest historical contribution.
