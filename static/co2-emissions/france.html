Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does France emit?

France emitted 264.2 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.7% of global emissions. That is about 8 tonnes every second.

What are France’s CO₂ emissions per person?

France emits 4.0 tonnes per person, 16% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (66.5 million people in 2024).

Are France’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 2.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 37%. Emissions peaked in 1973 at 542.6 million tonnes and are now 51% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in France?

Oil is the largest source, at 65% of France’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (170.7 million tonnes).

How much has France contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, France has emitted about 40 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 2.2% of the world total, the 8th largest historical contribution.
