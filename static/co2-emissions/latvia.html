How much CO₂ does Latvia emit?

What are Latvia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Latvia emits 3.5 tonnes per person, 27% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (1.9 million people in 2024).

Are Latvia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 1.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 17%. Emissions peaked in 1985 at 25.8 million tonnes and are now 75% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Latvia?

Oil is the largest source, at 66% of Latvia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (4.2 million tonnes).
