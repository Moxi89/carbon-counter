Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Romania emit?

Romania emitted 68.6 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Romania’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Romania emits 3.6 tonnes per person, 24% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (19.0 million people in 2024).

Are Romania’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.0% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 32%. Emissions peaked in 1989 at 213.0 million tonnes and are now 68% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Romania?

Oil is the largest source, at 50% of Romania’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (34.5 million tonnes).

How much has Romania contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Romania has emitted about 9 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.5% of the world total, the 29th largest historical contribution.
