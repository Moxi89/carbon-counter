Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Vietnam emit?

Vietnam emitted 370.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.0% of global emissions. That is about 12 tonnes every second.

What are Vietnam’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Vietnam emits 3.7 tonnes per person, 22% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (101.0 million people in 2024).

Are Vietnam’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 6.8% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 287%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Vietnam?

Coal is the largest source, at 62% of Vietnam’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (229.2 million tonnes).

How much has Vietnam contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Vietnam has emitted about 6 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 38th largest historical contribution.
