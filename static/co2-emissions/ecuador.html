Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Ecuador emit?

Ecuador emitted 46.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Ecuador’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Ecuador emits 2.5 tonnes per person, 46% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (18.1 million people in 2024).

Are Ecuador’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 3.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 50%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Ecuador?

Oil is the largest source, at 86% of Ecuador’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (39.5 million tonnes).

How much has Ecuador contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Ecuador has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 77th largest historical contribution.
