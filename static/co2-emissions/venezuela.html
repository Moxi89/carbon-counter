Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Venezuela emit?

Venezuela emitted 116.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.3% of global emissions. That is about 4 tonnes every second.

What are Venezuela’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Venezuela emits 4.1 tonnes per person, 14% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (28.4 million people in 2024).

Are Venezuela’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 3.0% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 22%. Emissions peaked in 2013 at 198.5 million tonnes and are now 42% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Venezuela?

Oil is the largest source, at 48% of Venezuela’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (56.2 million tonnes).

How much has Venezuela contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Venezuela has emitted about 8 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.4% of the world total, the 31st largest historical contribution.
