Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Ghana emit?

Ghana emitted 21.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Ghana’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Ghana emits 0.6 tonnes per person, 87% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (34.4 million people in 2024).

Are Ghana’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 4.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 244%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Ghana?

Oil is the largest source, at 60% of Ghana’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (12.5 million tonnes).
