How much CO₂ does Mauritius emit?

What are Mauritius’ CO₂ emissions per person?

Mauritius emits 3.7 tonnes per person, 22% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (1.3 million people in 2024).

Are Mauritius’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 2.2% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 42%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Mauritius?

Oil is the largest source, at 66% of Mauritius’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (3.1 million tonnes).

What are Mauritius’ consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 7.9 million tonnes in 2023, 72% more than the 4.6 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.
