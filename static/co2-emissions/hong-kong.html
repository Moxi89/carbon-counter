Hong Kong in context

0.09% vs 0.09%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

Hong Kong is home to 0.09% of the world’s population and produces 0.09% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is smaller than its share of people.

+23%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 23% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (27.1 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

144 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, Hong Kong was responsible for 144.2 Mt in 2023, 337% more than the 33.0 Mt produced at home: it is a net importer of emissions.

122 g

CO₂ per kWh of energy

Each kilowatt-hour of primary energy used in Hong Kong came with about 122 g of CO₂ in 2024 (world: 218 g), pointing to a much cleaner energy mix than the world average.

Its economy emitted 0.09 kg of CO₂ for each dollar of output in 2022 (adjusted for purchasing power), 70% below the world average of 0.29 kg.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Hong Kong emit?

Hong Kong emitted 33.3 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Hong Kong’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Hong Kong emits 4.5 tonnes per person, 5% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (7.4 million people in 2024).

Are Hong Kong’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.9% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 23%. Emissions peaked in 2014 at 45.5 million tonnes and are now 27% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Hong Kong?

Coal is the largest source, at 34% of Hong Kong’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (11.3 million tonnes).

What are Hong Kong’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 144.2 million tonnes in 2023, 337% more than the 33.0 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Hong Kong contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Hong Kong has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 72nd largest historical contribution.
