Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Japan emit?

Japan emitted 961.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 2.5% of global emissions. That is about 31 tonnes every second.

What are Japan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Japan emits 7.8 tonnes per person, 1.6× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (123.8 million people in 2024).

Are Japan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 2.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 25%. Emissions peaked in 2013 at 1.31 billion tonnes and are now 27% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Japan?

Coal is the largest source, at 40% of Japan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (381.0 million tonnes).

How much has Japan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Japan has emitted about 70 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 3.8% of the world total, the 6th largest historical contribution.
