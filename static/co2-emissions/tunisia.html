Tunisia in context

0.15% vs 0.08%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

Tunisia is home to 0.15% of the world’s population and produces 0.08% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is smaller than its share of people.

+124%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 124% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (14.6 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

30 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, Tunisia was responsible for 30.2 Mt in 2023, 5% less than the 31.7 Mt produced at home: part of its output is consumed elsewhere.

265 g

CO₂ per kWh of energy

Each kilowatt-hour of primary energy used in Tunisia came with about 265 g of CO₂ in 2024 (world: 218 g), pointing to a more fossil-heavy energy mix than the world average.

0.24 kg

CO₂ per dollar of GDP

Its economy emitted 0.24 kg of CO₂ for each dollar of output in 2022 (adjusted for purchasing power), 17% below the world average of 0.29 kg.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Tunisia emit?

Tunisia emitted 32.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Tunisia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Tunisia emits 2.7 tonnes per person, 44% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (12.3 million people in 2024).

Are Tunisia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 3.2% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 45%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Tunisia?

Oil is the largest source, at 47% of Tunisia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (15.2 million tonnes).

What are Tunisia’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 30.2 million tonnes in 2023, 5% less than the 31.7 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because part of what it produces is exported and consumed in other countries. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Tunisia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Tunisia has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 81st largest historical contribution.
