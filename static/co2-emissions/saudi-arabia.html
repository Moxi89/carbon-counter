Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Saudi Arabia emit?

Saudi Arabia emitted 692.1 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.8% of global emissions. That is about 22 tonnes every second.

What are Saudi Arabia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Saudi Arabia emits 20.4 tonnes per person, 4.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (34.0 million people in 2024).

Are Saudi Arabia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 2.2% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 68%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 708.3 million tonnes and are now 2% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Saudi Arabia?

Oil is the largest source, at 65% of Saudi Arabia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (451.5 million tonnes).

How much has Saudi Arabia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Saudi Arabia has emitted about 19 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.0% of the world total, the 18th largest historical contribution.
