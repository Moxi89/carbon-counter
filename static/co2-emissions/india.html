Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does India emit?

India emitted 3.19 billion tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 8.3% of global emissions. That is about 101 tonnes every second.

What are India’s CO₂ emissions per person?

India emits 2.2 tonnes per person, 53% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (1,450.9 million people in 2024).

Are India’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 4.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 167%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in India?

Coal is the largest source, at 66% of India’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (2.11 billion tonnes).

How much has India contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, India has emitted about 66 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 3.6% of the world total, the 7th largest historical contribution.
