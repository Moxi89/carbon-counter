Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Portugal emit?

Portugal emitted 35.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Portugal’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Portugal emits 3.4 tonnes per person, 28% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (10.4 million people in 2024).

Are Portugal’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 5.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Emissions peaked in 2005 at 69.6 million tonnes and are now 49% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Portugal?

Oil is the largest source, at 69% of Portugal’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (24.7 million tonnes).

How much has Portugal contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Portugal has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 58th largest historical contribution.
