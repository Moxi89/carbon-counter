Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does North Korea emit?

North Korea emitted 62.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are North Korea’s CO₂ emissions per person?

North Korea emits 2.4 tonnes per person, 50% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (26.5 million people in 2024).

Are North Korea’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.8% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 17%. Emissions peaked in 1989 at 209.3 million tonnes and are now 70% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in North Korea?

Coal is the largest source, at 91% of North Korea’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (57.2 million tonnes).

How much has North Korea contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, North Korea has emitted about 5 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 39th largest historical contribution.
