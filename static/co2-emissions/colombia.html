Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Colombia emit?

Colombia emitted 92.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 3 tonnes every second.

What are Colombia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Colombia emits 1.8 tonnes per person, 63% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (52.9 million people in 2024).

Are Colombia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 3.0% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 54%. Emissions peaked in 2021 at 103.7 million tonnes and are now 11% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Colombia?

Oil is the largest source, at 45% of Colombia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (42.1 million tonnes).

How much has Colombia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Colombia has emitted about 4 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 52nd largest historical contribution.
