Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Sudan emit?

Sudan emitted 17.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Sudan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Sudan emits 0.3 tonnes per person, 93% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (50.4 million people in 2024).

Are Sudan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 68%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 22.0 million tonnes and are now 19% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Sudan?

Oil is the largest source, at 100% of Sudan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (17.8 million tonnes).

How much has Sudan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Sudan has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.0% of the world total, the 102nd largest historical contribution.
