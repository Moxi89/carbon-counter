Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Kuwait emit?

Kuwait emitted 129.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.3% of global emissions. That is about 4 tonnes every second.

What are Kuwait’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Kuwait emits 26.2 tonnes per person, 5.5× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (4.9 million people in 2024).

Are Kuwait’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 5.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 74%. Emissions peaked in 1991 at 493.0 million tonnes and are now 74% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Kuwait?

Oil is the largest source, at 56% of Kuwait’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (72.5 million tonnes).

How much has Kuwait contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Kuwait has emitted about 4 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 51st largest historical contribution.
