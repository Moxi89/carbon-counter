Argentina in context

0.56% vs 0.44%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

Argentina is home to 0.56% of the world’s population and produces 0.44% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is smaller than its share of people.

+52%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 52% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (112.2 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

186 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, Argentina was responsible for 185.7 Mt in 2023, 4% more than the 178.4 Mt produced at home: it is a net importer of emissions.

174 g

CO₂ per kWh of energy

Each kilowatt-hour of primary energy used in Argentina came with about 174 g of CO₂ in 2024 (world: 218 g), pointing to a cleaner energy mix than the world average.

Its economy emitted 0.21 kg of CO₂ for each dollar of output in 2022 (adjusted for purchasing power), 25% below the world average of 0.29 kg.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Argentina emit?

Argentina emitted 171.1 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.4% of global emissions. That is about 5 tonnes every second.

What are Argentina’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Argentina emits 3.7 tonnes per person, 21% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (45.7 million people in 2024).

Are Argentina’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 4.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 6%. Emissions peaked in 2015 at 191.8 million tonnes and are now 11% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Argentina?

Gas is the largest source, at 52% of Argentina’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (89.2 million tonnes).

What are Argentina’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 185.7 million tonnes in 2023, 4% more than the 178.4 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Argentina contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Argentina has emitted about 9 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.5% of the world total, the 28th largest historical contribution.
