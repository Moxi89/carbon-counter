Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Bangladesh emit?

Bangladesh emitted 108.3 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.3% of global emissions. That is about 3 tonnes every second.

What are Bangladesh’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Bangladesh emits 0.6 tonnes per person, 87% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (173.6 million people in 2024).

Are Bangladesh’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 3.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 187%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Bangladesh?

Gas is the largest source, at 52% of Bangladesh’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (56.0 million tonnes).

How much has Bangladesh contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Bangladesh has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 70th largest historical contribution.
