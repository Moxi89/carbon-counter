Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Algeria emit?

Algeria emitted 198.2 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.5% of global emissions. That is about 6 tonnes every second.

What are Algeria’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Algeria emits 4.2 tonnes per person, 11% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (46.8 million people in 2024).

Are Algeria’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 2.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 81%. Emissions peaked in 2023 at 202.8 million tonnes and are now 2% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Algeria?

Gas is the largest source, at 51% of Algeria’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (101.8 million tonnes).

How much has Algeria contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Algeria has emitted about 5 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 40th largest historical contribution.
