United Arab Emirates in context

0.14% vs 0.58%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

The United Arab Emirates is home to 0.14% of the world’s population and produces 0.58% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is larger than its share of people.

+328%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 328% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (51.9 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

248 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, the United Arab Emirates was responsible for 248.1 Mt in 2023, 18% more than the 210.4 Mt produced at home: it is a net importer of emissions.

146 g

CO₂ per kWh of energy

Each kilowatt-hour of primary energy used in the United Arab Emirates came with about 146 g of CO₂ in 2024 (world: 218 g), pointing to a much cleaner energy mix than the world average.

0.27 kg

CO₂ per dollar of GDP

Its economy emitted 0.27 kg of CO₂ for each dollar of output in 2022 (adjusted for purchasing power), close to the world average of 0.29 kg.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does the United Arab Emirates emit?

The United Arab Emirates emitted 222.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.6% of global emissions. That is about 7 tonnes every second.

What are the United Arab Emirates’ CO₂ emissions per person?

The United Arab Emirates emits 20.1 tonnes per person, 4.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (11.0 million people in 2024).

Are the United Arab Emirates’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 5.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 93%. Emissions peaked in 2016 at 227.9 million tonnes and are now 3% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in the United Arab Emirates?

Gas is the largest source, at 65% of the United Arab Emirates’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (144.5 million tonnes).

What are the United Arab Emirates’ consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 248.1 million tonnes in 2023, 18% more than the 210.4 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has the United Arab Emirates contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, the United Arab Emirates has emitted about 6 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 35th largest historical contribution.
