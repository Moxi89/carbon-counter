Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Lithuania emit?

Lithuania emitted 12.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.0% of global emissions. That is about 0 tonnes every second.

What are Lithuania’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Lithuania emits 4.4 tonnes per person, 7% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (2.9 million people in 2024).

Are Lithuania’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (+0.0% on 2023). Compared with 2005, emissions are down 10%. Emissions peaked in 1985 at 40.5 million tonnes and are now 69% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Lithuania?

Oil is the largest source, at 67% of Lithuania’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (8.4 million tonnes).

What are Lithuania’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 20.5 million tonnes in 2023, 64% more than the 12.5 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Lithuania contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Lithuania has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 76th largest historical contribution.
