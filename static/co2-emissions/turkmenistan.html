Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Turkmenistan emit?

Turkmenistan emitted 81.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 3 tonnes every second.

What are Turkmenistan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Turkmenistan emits 10.8 tonnes per person, 2.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (7.5 million people in 2024).

Are Turkmenistan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 15.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 69%. Emissions peaked in 2022 at 102.1 million tonnes and are now 21% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Turkmenistan?

Gas is the largest source, at 77% of Turkmenistan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (62.4 million tonnes).

How much has Turkmenistan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Turkmenistan has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 59th largest historical contribution.
