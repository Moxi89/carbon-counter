Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Azerbaijan emit?

Azerbaijan emitted 39.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Azerbaijan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Azerbaijan emits 3.9 tonnes per person, 19% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (10.3 million people in 2024).

Are Azerbaijan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 6.8% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 16%. Emissions peaked in 1992 at 56.2 million tonnes and are now 29% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Azerbaijan?

Gas is the largest source, at 63% of Azerbaijan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (25.1 million tonnes).

How much has Azerbaijan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Azerbaijan has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 60th largest historical contribution.
