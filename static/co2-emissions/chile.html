Chile in context

0.24% vs 0.20%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

Chile is home to 0.24% of the world’s population and produces 0.20% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is smaller than its share of people.

+139%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 139% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (32.9 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

88 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, Chile was responsible for 88.2 Mt in 2023, 14% more than the 77.6 Mt produced at home: it is a net importer of emissions.

152 g

CO₂ per kWh of energy

Each kilowatt-hour of primary energy used in Chile came with about 152 g of CO₂ in 2024 (world: 218 g), pointing to a much cleaner energy mix than the world average.

0.19 kg

CO₂ per dollar of GDP

Its economy emitted 0.19 kg of CO₂ for each dollar of output in 2022 (adjusted for purchasing power), 34% below the world average of 0.29 kg.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Chile emit?

Chile emitted 78.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Chile’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Chile emits 4.0 tonnes per person, 16% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (19.8 million people in 2024).

Are Chile’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.4% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 29%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 92.0 million tonnes and are now 14% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Chile?

Oil is the largest source, at 69% of Chile’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (54.4 million tonnes).

What are Chile’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 88.2 million tonnes in 2023, 14% more than the 77.6 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Chile contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Chile has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 53rd largest historical contribution.
