Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Chile emit?

Chile emitted 78.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Chile’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Chile emits 4.0 tonnes per person, 16% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (19.8 million people in 2024).

Are Chile’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.4% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 29%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 92.0 million tonnes and are now 14% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Chile?

Oil is the largest source, at 69% of Chile’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (54.4 million tonnes).

How much has Chile contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Chile has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 53rd largest historical contribution.
