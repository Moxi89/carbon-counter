Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Nigeria emit?

Nigeria emitted 135.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.3% of global emissions. That is about 4 tonnes every second.

What are Nigeria’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Nigeria emits 0.6 tonnes per person, 88% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (232.7 million people in 2024).

Are Nigeria’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 4.9% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 34%. Emissions peaked in 2021 at 147.8 million tonnes and are now 8% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Nigeria?

Oil is the largest source, at 51% of Nigeria’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (68.9 million tonnes).

How much has Nigeria contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Nigeria has emitted about 5 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 45th largest historical contribution.
