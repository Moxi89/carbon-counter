Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Poland emit?

Poland emitted 272.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.7% of global emissions. That is about 9 tonnes every second.

What are Poland’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Poland emits 7.1 tonnes per person, 1.5× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (38.5 million people in 2024).

Are Poland’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 3.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 15%. Emissions peaked in 1987 at 463.7 million tonnes and are now 41% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Poland?

Coal is the largest source, at 47% of Poland’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (127.3 million tonnes).

How much has Poland contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Poland has emitted about 29 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.6% of the world total, the 11th largest historical contribution.
