Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does the Netherlands emit?

The Netherlands emitted 114.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.3% of global emissions. That is about 4 tonnes every second.

What are the Netherlands’ CO₂ emissions per person?

The Netherlands emits 6.3 tonnes per person, 1.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (18.2 million people in 2024).

Are the Netherlands’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 1.9% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 36%. Emissions peaked in 1979 at 187.0 million tonnes and are now 39% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in the Netherlands?

Gas is the largest source, at 47% of the Netherlands’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (53.7 million tonnes).

How much has the Netherlands contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, the Netherlands has emitted about 12 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.7% of the world total, the 26th largest historical contribution.
