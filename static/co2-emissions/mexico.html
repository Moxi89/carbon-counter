Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Mexico emit?

Mexico emitted 461.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.2% of global emissions. That is about 15 tonnes every second.

What are Mexico’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Mexico emits 3.5 tonnes per person, 26% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (130.9 million people in 2024).

Are Mexico’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 1%. Emissions peaked in 2012 at 501.5 million tonnes and are now 8% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Mexico?

Oil is the largest source, at 48% of Mexico’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (219.4 million tonnes).

How much has Mexico contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Mexico has emitted about 22 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.2% of the world total, the 14th largest historical contribution.
