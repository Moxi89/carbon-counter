0.10% vs 0.06%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 4,780% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (0.5 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Laos emit?

Laos emitted 24.4 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Laos’ CO₂ emissions per person?

Laos emits 3.1 tonnes per person, 34% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (7.8 million people in 2024).

Are Laos’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.8% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 1777%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Laos?

Coal is the largest source, at 68% of Laos’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (16.7 million tonnes).

What are Laos’ consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 14.8 million tonnes in 2023, 39% less than the 24.2 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because part of what it produces is exported and consumed in other countries. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Laos contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Laos has emitted about 0 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.0% of the world total, the 123rd largest historical contribution.
