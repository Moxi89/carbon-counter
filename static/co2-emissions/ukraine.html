Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Ukraine emit?

Ukraine emitted 142.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.4% of global emissions. That is about 5 tonnes every second.

What are Ukraine’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Ukraine emits 3.8 tonnes per person, 21% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (37.9 million people in 2024).

Are Ukraine’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 2.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 55%. Emissions peaked in 1985 at 743.7 million tonnes and are now 81% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Ukraine?

Coal is the largest source, at 38% of Ukraine’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (54.6 million tonnes).

How much has Ukraine contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Ukraine has emitted about 31 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.7% of the world total, the 10th largest historical contribution.
