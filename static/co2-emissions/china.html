Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does China emit?

China emitted 12.29 billion tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 31.8% of global emissions. That is about 390 tonnes every second.

What are China’s CO₂ emissions per person?

China emits 8.7 tonnes per person, 1.8× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (1,419.3 million people in 2024).

Are China’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.0% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 109%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in China?

Coal is the largest source, at 72% of China’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (8.89 billion tonnes).

How much has China contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, China has emitted about 285 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 15.4% of the world total, the 2nd largest historical contribution.
