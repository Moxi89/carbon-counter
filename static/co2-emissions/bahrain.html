Bahrain in context

0.02% vs 0.10%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

Bahrain is home to 0.02% of the world’s population and produces 0.10% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is larger than its share of people.

+239%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 239% higher than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (11.5 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

22 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, Bahrain was responsible for 22.1 Mt in 2023, 43% less than the 38.8 Mt produced at home: part of its output is consumed elsewhere.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Bahrain emit?

Bahrain emitted 39.0 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Bahrain’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Bahrain emits 24.3 tonnes per person, 5.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (1.6 million people in 2024).

Are Bahrain’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 59%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Bahrain?

Gas is the largest source, at 87% of Bahrain’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (33.8 million tonnes).

What are Bahrain’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 22.1 million tonnes in 2023, 43% less than the 38.8 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because part of what it produces is exported and consumed in other countries. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Bahrain contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Bahrain has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 80th largest historical contribution.
