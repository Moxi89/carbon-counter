Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Mongolia emit?

Mongolia emitted 44.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Mongolia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Mongolia emits 12.9 tonnes per person, 2.7× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (3.5 million people in 2024).

Are Mongolia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 426%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 47.1 million tonnes and are now 5% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Mongolia?

Coal is the largest source, at 87% of Mongolia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (38.7 million tonnes).

How much has Mongolia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Mongolia has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 87th largest historical contribution.
