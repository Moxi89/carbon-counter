Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Malaysia emit?

Malaysia emitted 290.2 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.8% of global emissions. That is about 9 tonnes every second.

What are Malaysia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Malaysia emits 8.2 tonnes per person, 1.7× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (35.6 million people in 2024).

Are Malaysia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 5.0% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 71%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Malaysia?

Coal is the largest source, at 34% of Malaysia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (97.6 million tonnes).

How much has Malaysia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Malaysia has emitted about 7 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.4% of the world total, the 33rd largest historical contribution.
