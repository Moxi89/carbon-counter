What are Honduras’ CO₂ emissions per person?

Honduras emits 1.2 tonnes per person, 75% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (10.8 million people in 2024).

Are Honduras’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 4.0% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 90%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Honduras?

Oil is the largest source, at 95% of Honduras’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (12.1 million tonnes).

What are Honduras’ consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 10.5 million tonnes in 2023, 15% less than the 12.4 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because part of what it produces is exported and consumed in other countries. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.
