Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Italy emit?

Italy emitted 301.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.8% of global emissions. That is about 10 tonnes every second.

What are Italy’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Italy emits 5.1 tonnes per person, 1.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (59.3 million people in 2024).

Are Italy’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 3.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Emissions peaked in 2005 at 501.4 million tonnes and are now 40% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Italy?

Oil is the largest source, at 51% of Italy’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (154.4 million tonnes).

How much has Italy contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Italy has emitted about 26 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.4% of the world total, the 12th largest historical contribution.
