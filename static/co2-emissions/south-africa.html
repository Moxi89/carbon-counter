Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does South Africa emit?

South Africa emitted 439.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.1% of global emissions. That is about 14 tonnes every second.

What are South Africa’s CO₂ emissions per person?

South Africa emits 6.9 tonnes per person, 1.5× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (64.0 million people in 2024).

Are South Africa’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.7% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 6%. Emissions peaked in 2008 at 494.7 million tonnes and are now 11% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in South Africa?

Coal is the largest source, at 84% of South Africa’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (371.4 million tonnes).

How much has South Africa contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, South Africa has emitted about 23 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.2% of the world total, the 13th largest historical contribution.
