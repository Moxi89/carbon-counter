Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Uzbekistan emit?

Uzbekistan emitted 139.2 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.4% of global emissions. That is about 4 tonnes every second.

What are Uzbekistan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Uzbekistan emits 3.8 tonnes per person, 19% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (36.4 million people in 2024).

Are Uzbekistan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 13.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 14%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Uzbekistan?

Gas is the largest source, at 74% of Uzbekistan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (103.6 million tonnes).

How much has Uzbekistan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Uzbekistan has emitted about 7 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.4% of the world total, the 34th largest historical contribution.
