Finland in context

0.07% vs 0.08%

Share of people vs share of CO₂

Finland is home to 0.07% of the world’s population and produces 0.08% of its fossil CO₂, so its share of emissions is larger than its share of people.

−48%

Change since 1990

Emissions in 2024 were 48% lower than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (56.9 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

48 Mt

Consumption-based CO₂ (2023)

Counting the CO₂ in goods it imports and exports, Finland was responsible for 47.5 Mt in 2023, 49% more than the 31.8 Mt produced at home: it is a net importer of emissions.

89 g

CO₂ per kWh of energy

Each kilowatt-hour of primary energy used in Finland came with about 89 g of CO₂ in 2024 (world: 218 g), pointing to a much cleaner energy mix than the world average.

0.16 kg

CO₂ per dollar of GDP

Its economy emitted 0.16 kg of CO₂ for each dollar of output in 2022 (adjusted for purchasing power), 44% below the world average of 0.29 kg.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Finland emit?

Finland emitted 29.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Finland’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Finland emits 5.3 tonnes per person, 1.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (5.6 million people in 2024).

Are Finland’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 6.3% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 48%. Emissions peaked in 2003 at 72.7 million tonnes and are now 59% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Finland?

Oil is the largest source, at 61% of Finland’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (18.2 million tonnes).

What are Finland’s consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 47.5 million tonnes in 2023, 49% more than the 31.8 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.

How much has Finland contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Finland has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 54th largest historical contribution.
