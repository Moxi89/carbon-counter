Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Thailand emit?

Thailand emitted 267.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.7% of global emissions. That is about 8 tonnes every second.

What are Thailand’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Thailand emits 3.7 tonnes per person, 21% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (71.7 million people in 2024).

Are Thailand’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.2% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 25%. Emissions peaked in 2018 at 288.3 million tonnes and are now 7% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Thailand?

Oil is the largest source, at 39% of Thailand’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (105.3 million tonnes).

How much has Thailand contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Thailand has emitted about 8 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.5% of the world total, the 30th largest historical contribution.
