Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Morocco emit?

Morocco emitted 69.1 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Morocco’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Morocco emits 1.8 tonnes per person, 62% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (38.1 million people in 2024).

Are Morocco’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (+0.1% on 2023). Compared with 2005, emissions are up 55%. Emissions peaked in 2021 at 70.3 million tonnes and are now 2% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Morocco?

Oil is the largest source, at 58% of Morocco’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (39.9 million tonnes).

How much has Morocco contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Morocco has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 68th largest historical contribution.
