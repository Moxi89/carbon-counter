Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Dominican Republic emit?

Dominican Republic emitted 33.1 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Dominican Republic’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Dominican Republic emits 2.9 tonnes per person, 39% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (11.4 million people in 2024).

Are Dominican Republic’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 4.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 85%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Dominican Republic?

Oil is the largest source, at 58% of Dominican Republic’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (19.4 million tonnes).

How much has Dominican Republic contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Dominican Republic has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 86th largest historical contribution.
