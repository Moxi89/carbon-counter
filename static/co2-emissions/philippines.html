Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does the Philippines emit?

The Philippines emitted 174.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.5% of global emissions. That is about 6 tonnes every second.

What are the Philippines’ CO₂ emissions per person?

The Philippines emits 1.5 tonnes per person, 68% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (115.8 million people in 2024).

Are the Philippines’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 8.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 142%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in the Philippines?

Coal is the largest source, at 53% of the Philippines’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (92.1 million tonnes).

How much has the Philippines contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, the Philippines has emitted about 4 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 50th largest historical contribution.
