Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does the United Kingdom emit?

The United Kingdom emitted 312.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.8% of global emissions. That is about 10 tonnes every second.

What are the United Kingdom’s CO₂ emissions per person?

The United Kingdom emits 4.5 tonnes per person, close to the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (69.1 million people in 2024).

Are the United Kingdom’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.7% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 45%. Emissions peaked in 1971 at 660.4 million tonnes and are now 53% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in the United Kingdom?

Oil is the largest source, at 51% of the United Kingdom’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (159.6 million tonnes).

How much has the United Kingdom contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, the United Kingdom has emitted about 80 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 4.3% of the world total, the 5th largest historical contribution.
