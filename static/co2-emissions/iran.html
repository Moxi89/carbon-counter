Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Iran emit?

Iran emitted 792.6 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 2.0% of global emissions. That is about 25 tonnes every second.

What are Iran’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Iran emits 8.7 tonnes per person, 1.8× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (91.6 million people in 2024).

Are Iran’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (+0.4% on 2023). Compared with 2005, emissions are up 70%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Iran?

Gas is the largest source, at 62% of Iran’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (492.7 million tonnes).

How much has Iran contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Iran has emitted about 22 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.2% of the world total, the 15th largest historical contribution.
