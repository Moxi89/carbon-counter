Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Iraq emit?

Iraq emitted 233.6 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.6% of global emissions. That is about 7 tonnes every second.

What are Iraq’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Iraq emits 5.1 tonnes per person, 1.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (46.0 million people in 2024).

Are Iraq’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 2.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 104%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Iraq?

Oil is the largest source, at 63% of Iraq’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (147.5 million tonnes).

How much has Iraq contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Iraq has emitted about 5 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 41st largest historical contribution.
