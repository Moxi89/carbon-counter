Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Pakistan emit?

Pakistan emitted 179.8 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.5% of global emissions. That is about 6 tonnes every second.

What are Pakistan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Pakistan emits 0.7 tonnes per person, 85% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (251.3 million people in 2024).

Are Pakistan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 3.8% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 38%. Emissions peaked in 2021 at 243.6 million tonnes and are now 26% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Pakistan?

Gas is the largest source, at 33% of Pakistan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (59.9 million tonnes).

How much has Pakistan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Pakistan has emitted about 6 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 37th largest historical contribution.
