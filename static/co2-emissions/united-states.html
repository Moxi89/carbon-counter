Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does the United States emit?

The United States emitted 4.90 billion tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 12.7% of global emissions. That is about 156 tonnes every second.

What are the United States’ CO₂ emissions per person?

The United States emits 14.2 tonnes per person, 3.0× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (345.4 million people in 2024).

Are the United States’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (-0.3% on 2023). Emissions peaked in 2005 at 6.13 billion tonnes and are now 20% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in the United States?

Oil is the largest source, at 45% of the United States’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (2.19 billion tonnes).

How much has the United States contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, the United States has emitted about 435 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 23.5% of the world total, the largest historical contribution of any country.
