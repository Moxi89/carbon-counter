Emissions in 2024 were 3% lower than in 1990, the baseline year of the Kyoto Protocol (19.9 Mt then). World emissions rose 70% over the same period.

Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Bosnia and Herzegovina emit?

Bosnia and Herzegovina emitted 19.4 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Bosnia and Herzegovina’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Bosnia and Herzegovina emits 6.1 tonnes per person, 1.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (3.2 million people in 2024).

Are Bosnia and Herzegovina’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 2.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 21%. Emissions peaked in 2011 at 23.8 million tonnes and are now 18% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Bosnia and Herzegovina?

Coal is the largest source, at 71% of Bosnia and Herzegovina’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (13.9 million tonnes).

How much has Bosnia and Herzegovina contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Bosnia and Herzegovina has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 82nd largest historical contribution.
