Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Switzerland emit?

Switzerland emitted 32.1 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Switzerland’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Switzerland emits 3.6 tonnes per person, 24% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (8.9 million people in 2024).

Are Switzerland’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (+0.3% on 2023). Compared with 2005, emissions are down 30%. Emissions peaked in 1973 at 46.2 million tonnes and are now 31% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Switzerland?

Oil is the largest source, at 76% of Switzerland’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (24.4 million tonnes).

How much has Switzerland contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Switzerland has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 55th largest historical contribution.
