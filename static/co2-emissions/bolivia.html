Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Bolivia emit?

Bolivia emitted 28.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Bolivia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Bolivia emits 2.3 tonnes per person, 51% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (12.4 million people in 2024).

Are Bolivia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 16.7% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 133%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Bolivia?

Oil is the largest source, at 51% of Bolivia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (14.5 million tonnes).

How much has Bolivia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Bolivia has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.0% of the world total, the 96th largest historical contribution.
