Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Czechia emit?

Czechia emitted 75.6 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Czechia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Czechia emits 7.0 tonnes per person, 1.5× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (10.7 million people in 2024).

Are Czechia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 9.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 40%. Emissions peaked in 1978 at 187.5 million tonnes and are now 60% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Czechia?

Coal is the largest source, at 46% of Czechia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (34.4 million tonnes).

How much has Czechia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Czechia has emitted about 12 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.7% of the world total, the 25th largest historical contribution.
