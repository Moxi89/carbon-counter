Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Austria emit?

Austria emitted 56.4 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Austria’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Austria emits 6.2 tonnes per person, 1.3× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (9.1 million people in 2024).

Are Austria’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 0.9% in 2024 compared with 2023. Emissions peaked in 2005 at 79.1 million tonnes and are now 29% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Austria?

Oil is the largest source, at 51% of Austria’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (28.9 million tonnes).

How much has Austria contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Austria has emitted about 6 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.3% of the world total, the 36th largest historical contribution.
