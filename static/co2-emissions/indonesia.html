Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Indonesia emit?

Indonesia emitted 812.2 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 2.1% of global emissions. That is about 26 tonnes every second.

What are Indonesia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Indonesia emits 2.9 tonnes per person, 39% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (283.5 million people in 2024).

Are Indonesia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 6.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 134%. 2024 was the highest year on record.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Indonesia?

Coal is the largest source, at 54% of Indonesia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (436.1 million tonnes).

How much has Indonesia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Indonesia has emitted about 17 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.9% of the world total, the 20th largest historical contribution.
