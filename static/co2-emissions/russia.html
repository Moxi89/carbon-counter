Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Russia emit?

Russia emitted 1.78 billion tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 4.6% of global emissions. That is about 56 tonnes every second.

What are Russia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Russia emits 12.3 tonnes per person, 2.6× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (144.8 million people in 2024).

Are Russia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 2.7% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 14%. Emissions peaked in 1990 at 2.54 billion tonnes and are now 30% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Russia?

Gas is the largest source, at 48% of Russia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (857.7 million tonnes).

How much has Russia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Russia has emitted about 123 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 6.6% of the world total, the 3rd largest historical contribution.
