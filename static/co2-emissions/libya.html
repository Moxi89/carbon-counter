Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Libya emit?

Libya emitted 65.3 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Libya’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Libya emits 8.8 tonnes per person, 1.9× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (7.4 million people in 2024).

Are Libya’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.7% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 17%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 73.8 million tonnes and are now 12% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Libya?

Oil is the largest source, at 57% of Libya’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (36.9 million tonnes).

How much has Libya contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Libya has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 63rd largest historical contribution.
