What are Cyprus’ CO₂ emissions per person?

Cyprus emits 5.4 tonnes per person, 1.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (1.4 million people in 2024).

Are Cyprus’ CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 1.4% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 9%. Emissions peaked in 2008 at 8.7 million tonnes and are now 16% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Cyprus?

Oil is the largest source, at 86% of Cyprus’ fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (6.3 million tonnes).

What are Cyprus’ consumption-based CO₂ emissions?

About 8.6 million tonnes in 2023, 20% more than the 7.2 million tonnes emitted within its borders, because it imports more carbon-intensive goods than it exports. Consumption-based accounting adds the emissions embodied in imports and subtracts those in exports.
