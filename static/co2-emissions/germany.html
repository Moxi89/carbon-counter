Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Germany emit?

Germany emitted 572.3 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.5% of global emissions. That is about 18 tonnes every second.

What are Germany’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Germany emits 6.8 tonnes per person, 1.4× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (84.6 million people in 2024).

Are Germany’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 3.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 34%. Emissions peaked in 1979 at 1.12 billion tonnes and are now 49% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Germany?

Oil is the largest source, at 41% of Germany’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (233.0 million tonnes).

How much has Germany contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Germany has emitted about 95 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 5.1% of the world total, the 4th largest historical contribution.
