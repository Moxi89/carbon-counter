Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Taiwan emit?

Taiwan emitted 262.3 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.7% of global emissions. That is about 8 tonnes every second.

What are Taiwan’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Taiwan emits 11.3 tonnes per person, 2.4× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (23.2 million people in 2024).

Are Taiwan’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 1.8% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 2%. Emissions peaked in 2017 at 285.7 million tonnes and are now 8% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Taiwan?

Coal is the largest source, at 54% of Taiwan’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (141.1 million tonnes).

How much has Taiwan contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Taiwan has emitted about 10 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.6% of the world total, the 27th largest historical contribution.
