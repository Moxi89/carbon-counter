Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Belgium emit?

Belgium emitted 85.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.2% of global emissions. That is about 3 tonnes every second.

What are Belgium’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Belgium emits 7.3 tonnes per person, 1.5× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (11.7 million people in 2024).

Are Belgium’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.9% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 32%. Emissions peaked in 1979 at 139.8 million tonnes and are now 39% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Belgium?

Oil is the largest source, at 49% of Belgium’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (42.0 million tonnes).

How much has Belgium contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Belgium has emitted about 13 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.7% of the world total, the 23rd largest historical contribution.
