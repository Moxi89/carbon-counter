Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does South Korea emit?

South Korea emitted 583.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.5% of global emissions. That is about 19 tonnes every second.

What are South Korea’s CO₂ emissions per person?

South Korea emits 11.3 tonnes per person, 2.4× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (51.7 million people in 2024).

Are South Korea’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 0.9% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 17%. Emissions peaked in 2018 at 670.2 million tonnes and are now 13% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in South Korea?

Coal is the largest source, at 42% of South Korea’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (247.5 million tonnes).

How much has South Korea contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, South Korea has emitted about 21 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.1% of the world total, the 16th largest historical contribution.
