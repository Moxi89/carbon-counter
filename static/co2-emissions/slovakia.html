Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Slovakia emit?

Slovakia emitted 29.1 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Slovakia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Slovakia emits 5.3 tonnes per person, 1.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (5.5 million people in 2024).

Are Slovakia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 5.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 32%. Emissions peaked in 1990 at 61.5 million tonnes and are now 53% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Slovakia?

Oil is the largest source, at 35% of Slovakia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (10.1 million tonnes).

How much has Slovakia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Slovakia has emitted about 4 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.2% of the world total, the 48th largest historical contribution.
