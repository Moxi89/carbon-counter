Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Australia emit?

Australia emitted 386.7 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 1.0% of global emissions. That is about 12 tonnes every second.

What are Australia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Australia emits 14.5 tonnes per person, 3.1× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (26.7 million people in 2024).

Are Australia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 0.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 0%. Emissions peaked in 2019 at 414.9 million tonnes and are now 7% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Australia?

Oil is the largest source, at 38% of Australia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (145.8 million tonnes).

How much has Australia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Australia has emitted about 20 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 1.1% of the world total, the 17th largest historical contribution.
