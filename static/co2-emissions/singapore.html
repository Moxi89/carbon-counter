Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Singapore emit?

Singapore emitted 53.9 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Singapore’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Singapore emits 9.2 tonnes per person, 2.0× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (5.8 million people in 2024).

Are Singapore’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions rose 5.5% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 36%. Emissions peaked in 1994 at 60.1 million tonnes and are now 10% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Singapore?

Oil is the largest source, at 59% of Singapore’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (31.8 million tonnes).

How much has Singapore contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Singapore has emitted about 2 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 65th largest historical contribution.
