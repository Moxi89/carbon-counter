Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Israel emit?

Israel emitted 52.6 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 2 tonnes every second.

What are Israel’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Israel emits 5.6 tonnes per person, 1.2× the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (9.4 million people in 2024).

Are Israel’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 5.1% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are down 7%. Emissions peaked in 2012 at 74.8 million tonnes and are now 30% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Israel?

Gas is the largest source, at 39% of Israel’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (20.4 million tonnes).

How much has Israel contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Israel has emitted about 3 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 61st largest historical contribution.
