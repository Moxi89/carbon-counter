Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Croatia emit?

Croatia emitted 18.5 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Croatia’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Croatia emits 4.8 tonnes per person, close to the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (3.9 million people in 2024).

Are Croatia’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions were roughly flat in 2024 (+0.0% on 2023). Compared with 2005, emissions are down 21%. Emissions peaked in 2007 at 24.8 million tonnes and are now 25% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Croatia?

Oil is the largest source, at 60% of Croatia’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (11.1 million tonnes).

How much has Croatia contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Croatia has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.1% of the world total, the 79th largest historical contribution.
