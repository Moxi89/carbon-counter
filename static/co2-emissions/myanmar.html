Questions and answers

How much CO₂ does Myanmar emit?

Myanmar emitted 31.6 million tonnes of CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry in 2024, 0.1% of global emissions. That is about 1 tonnes every second.

What are Myanmar’s CO₂ emissions per person?

Myanmar emits 0.6 tonnes per person, 88% below the world average of 4.7 t. Per-person emissions divide the national total by population (54.5 million people in 2024).

Are Myanmar’s CO₂ emissions rising or falling?

Emissions fell 0.6% in 2024 compared with 2023. Compared with 2005, emissions are up 175%. Emissions peaked in 2020 at 34.6 million tonnes and are now 9% lower.

What is the main source of CO₂ emissions in Myanmar?

Oil is the largest source, at 55% of Myanmar’s fossil and industry CO₂ in 2024 (17.3 million tonnes).

How much has Myanmar contributed to historical CO₂ emissions?

Since 1750, Myanmar has emitted about 1 billion tonnes of fossil CO₂, 0.0% of the world total, the 93rd largest historical contribution.
