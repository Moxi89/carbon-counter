Contact us

Corrections, data questions, press and reuse: here is how to reach us.

Updated 23 October 2026

Email

Email us: hello@carbonemissionscounter.com

We read every message. We can’t promise a personal reply to everything, but corrections and data questions always get a response.

What to contact us about

  • Corrections: tell us the page and the figure, ideally with a source. See our corrections policy.
  • Data and method questions: first check how the counter works, which covers most of them.
  • Press, education and reuse: you don’t need permission to quote figures with a link; see the terms of use and the embeddable widget.
  • Privacy requests: see the privacy policy.

What we can’t help with

We don’t offer consulting, carbon audits or offsets, and we don’t accept paid articles or link placements.
