Privacy policy

We collect as little as possible: no accounts, no ads, no tracking scripts and no cookies of our own.

Updated 24 October 2026

The short version

In short: no accounts, no advertising, no tracking scripts and no cookies of our own. The counters and the footprint calculator run in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to us. Our host sets one strictly necessary security cookie (see Cookies).

What we collect

We don’t ask for personal information: there are no sign-ups, forms or newsletters.

  • Server logs. Like any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, so it can deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We don’t use these logs to identify you.
  • Page-view statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate without cookies and without following you across websites.
  • Things you type. Your birth year in the lifetime tool and your answers in the footprint calculator stay in your browser. If you share a result, only the link you choose to share contains them.
  • Emails you send us. Messages to hello@carbonemissionscounter.com are forwarded by Cloudflare to our inbox. We see your email address and message, use them only to reply, keep them no longer than needed, and delete them if you ask.

Cookies

We don’t set any cookies ourselves, and the site stores nothing else in your browser. Our host, Cloudflare, sets one security cookie, cf_clearance, which helps tell real visitors from automated bots. Cloudflare classes it as strictly necessary: it holds no advertising or tracking profile, isn’t used to follow you across websites, and lasts up to a year. You can delete it in your browser at any time and the site will still work. Because it is strictly necessary, no cookie banner is needed. The embeddable counter widget sets no cookies of its own; Cloudflare’s security cookie can also be set when the widget is shown on another site.

If this changes, for example if we start showing ads, we will update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare: hosting, content delivery, security, email forwarding and privacy-friendly page-view statistics. Cloudflare is based in the United States; see its privacy policy for how data is protected when it is transferred there.
  • GitHub: storage of the website’s source code. No visitor data is stored there.

We do not use advertising networks, and we never sell or share data.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about visitors, apart from emails you choose to send us. To see, correct or delete those, or if you have a question, email hello@carbonemissionscounter.com. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Children can use the site safely: it collects nothing from them.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top.
