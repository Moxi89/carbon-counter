Questions and answers

Which country has the highest CO₂ emissions per person?

Among countries with more than one million people, Qatar has the highest fossil CO₂ emissions per person at 41.3 tonnes a year, followed by Kuwait (26.2 t) and Bahrain (24.3 t). Oil and gas production and very high energy use explain most of the top places.

What is the average CO₂ emissions per person in the world?

About 4.7 tonnes of fossil CO₂ per person in 2024. Including land-use change, the figure is a little over 5 tonnes.

How do the US, China and India compare per person?

The United States emits 14.2 t per person, China 8.7 t and India 2.2 t. China emits far more in total, but each American is responsible for roughly 1.6 times as much CO₂ as each person in China.

Which countries emit the least CO₂ per person?

The lowest per-person emissions are in low-income countries, many in sub-Saharan Africa, at well under 0.5 tonnes; Democratic Republic of Congo is at 0.05 t.
