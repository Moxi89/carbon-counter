Questions and answers

Which country has emitted the most CO₂ in history?

The United States has emitted the most fossil CO₂ since 1750: about 435 billion tonnes, 23.5% of the world total. China is second (15.4%) and Russia third (6.6%).

Why do historical emissions matter?

Warming depends on the total amount of CO₂ added to the atmosphere over time, because CO₂ stays there for centuries. That is why the carbon budget for 1.5 °C is expressed as a total, and why historical responsibility is part of climate negotiations.

How concentrated are historical emissions?

Very. The ten largest historical emitters account for about 68% of all fossil CO₂ emitted since 1750.

Do these figures include deforestation?

No. They cover fossil fuels and industry (coal, oil, gas, cement and flaring). Including land-use change would raise the totals of countries with large historical deforestation, such as Brazil and Indonesia.
