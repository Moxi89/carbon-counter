Download the data

Two CSV files, free to use under CC BY 4.0. They open in Excel, Google Sheets, R or Python.

CO₂ emissions by country213 countries plus the world, 2024: total, change, share, per person, population, cumulative since 1750, 1990 and 2005 levelsCSV · 24 KB CO₂ emissions over timeWorld from 1950 and the 159 countries with over 1 million people from 2000, to 2024CSV · 100 KB

Figures are annual CO₂ from fossil fuels and industry (cement and other processes), excluding land-use change, in million tonnes (Mt). Column notes are in the README. For the full dataset with more variables, go to the source: Our World in Data on GitHub.

How to cite this site

Carbon Emissions Counter (2026). CO₂ emissions by country, 2024 data. Based on Our World in Data and the Global Carbon Budget 2025. https://www.carbonemissionscounter.com/data

For the live global counter, cite the home page; for one country, cite its page, for example China.
