Estimates, not measurements

The counters are estimates calculated from published annual data, mainly the Global Carbon Budget and Our World in Data. They are not live measurements, and like all emissions data they carry uncertainty. How the counter works explains the method and its limits.

Everything on the site is for information and education. We work to keep it accurate, but we can’t guarantee it is complete or error-free, so please check primary sources before relying on a figure for an important decision.
